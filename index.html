down now → launch

    
This is the part that moves. Every open incident on those pages lands here, newest first, in the company's own words with a link to their post. Degraded parts without an incident show up too. A narrative is only worth something in its first minutes, so one click turns any of them into a coin kit.

    

  


  

    
03
is it down?

    
Not on the board? Type any site. WR1D knocks on it from the server, not from your browser, so your own connection is not the thing being tested. You get what it heard back: up or down, the HTTP status and how many milliseconds it took. If it's down, it becomes a kit.

06
faq

    

      
where does the data come from?
From each company's own public status page (their Statuspage JSON feed), read on the server every minute. A page that does not answer is shown as unreachable, never as fine.

      
is WR1D part of any of these companies?
No. WR1D is independent and not affiliated with any company on the board. Names are used only to say whose status page is being read.

      
does WR1D launch the coin for me?
You launch it. The launch page sends your image and details to pump.fun's own IPFS, PumpPortal's public API builds the transaction for your wallet, you sign it, and WR1D only forwards the signed bytes to Solana. It never holds funds or keys.
